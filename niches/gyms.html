Gyms & trainers

Gyms, boutique studios, personal trainers, martial arts, yoga and pilates studios. Use this to plan one filming session that feeds a month of reels. Swap the [brackets] for your own details.

Vertical, steady, ~5 secondsHold each shot still or move slowly. No zooming.
Lights on, fans offBright, even light. Quiet room for talking clips.
Talking clipsFace the camera, say one full sentence, pause 2 seconds.

  5. Beginner-friendly options for this movement.
  6. Equipment explainer: what it is, how to use it.
  7. Trial or intro offer details (exact terms).
  8. Member milestone (with permission, no promised results).
  9. Recovery and mobility tip.
  10. Community event or challenge announcement.

4Rules to follow

  • No guaranteed results ("lose 20 lbs in 30 days"). Results vary; say so if you show progress.
  • Don't give medical advice; suggest people check with a doctor before starting a new program.
  • Only film members who agreed to it. Keep a consent list.
  • Supplements or partner products: disclose any paid relationship.

General guidance, not legal advice. Check your industry's, state's and platform's current rules.
